Why we moved off the cloud

Posted on June 1, 2026 by Sam

Our monthly bill was $41,000. After moving to three racks in a colo, it is $9,500 including staff time.

We kept object storage in the cloud because egress for backups is cheap enough.

What went wrong

  • The first switch we bought was the wrong model.
  • We underestimated power density.
